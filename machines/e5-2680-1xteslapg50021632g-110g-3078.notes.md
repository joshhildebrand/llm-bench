## Read this before comparing these rows to anything

**This is a llama.cpp box, not an LM Studio box.** Every row here came from
`llama-server` out of the `fiveforge/llama.cpp` fork (branch `wp-g2`), driven by
`defiant/harness/qwen36_v100.py` rather than `bench.py`. Full method, provenance and
analysis: `defiant/benchmarks/2026-08-14-v100-qwen36-256k.md`.

**Do not compare against `e5-2680-cpuonly-63g-b105`.** Same chassis, but that id predates
the second CPU, the RAM upgrade *and* the GPU — it is single-socket, 63 GB, CPU-only.

**Every row is at the full native 262,144 context**, allocated at load. VRAM does not move
with depth (28,958 MiB at every depth in the champion config), so "256K resident" is a
property of all of these rows, not just the deep ones.

## What the labels mean

| label | what it is |
|---|---|
| `nomtp-champ-natural` | the previous champion — llama.cpp's **default `-ub 512`** |
| `nomtp-ub4096` | same engine, `-ub 4096 -b 8192`. **The current champion** |
| `nomtp-ub4096-rpb2` | champion + `GGML_CUDA_MMVQ_DENSE_RPB=2` (batch-1 GEMV row tiling) |
| `mtp-ub2048` / `nomtp-ub2048` | the MTP A/B — **both** arms at `-ub 2048`, because MTP does not fit at 4096 |

All arms run `GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_GRAPHS=1 GGML_CUDA_VOLTA_MOE_MMQ=1`, flash-attention on,
f16 KV, greedy sampling.

## The three things worth knowing

1. **`-ub` is the single biggest lever on this model and the default is wrong for it.**
   512 → 4096 is worth **+100.8% prefill at 4K** and **+36.3% at the full 257,744**, with
   decode unchanged. With 256 experts and 8 routed per token, a 512-token micro-batch
   already touches nearly every expert, so the weight reads amortise over 8× more tokens
   at 4096. `GGML_CUDA_VOLTA_MOE_MMQ` is worth **+117%** at a wide micro-batch versus the
   +64% previously recorded at the narrow one — **neither flag should be quoted without
   the other.**

2. **Decode is at the memory-bandwidth wall, and the wall is computable.** At 257,744
   tokens each decoded token reads 7,664 MiB (2,624 MiB of weights + 5,040 MiB of f16 KV).
   Against the 964.8 GB/s this card actually achieves, that is a **120.1 tok/s ceiling**.
   Measured 62.92 is **52% of roofline**. A 150 tok/s target is outside this card's physics
   at f16 KV.

3. **Concurrency at full 256K is exactly one stream.** `-np` splits `-c` across slots, so
   `-np 8 -c 262144` is eight 32K streams, not eight 256K streams. Two streams at the full
   context (`-np 2 -c 524288`) OOMs at load on the compute buffer. Even at shallow depth,
   aggregate throughput saturates at **two** streams; beyond that it buys nothing and
   fairness falls to 0.276 by eight.

## Recommended config

```sh
GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_GRAPHS=1 GGML_CUDA_VOLTA_MOE_MMQ=1 \
  llama-server -m Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf \
  -ngl 99 -c 262144 -np 1 -ub 4096 -b 8192
```

`GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_GRAPHS=1` is a crash workaround, and it is **free** — CUDA graphs never
engage for this model in either setting (identical launch counts, zero graph launches), so
it costs nothing to leave on. Add `--spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 3` **with
`-ub 2048`** for +29% decode at 256K, at the cost of −9.7% prefill, +86% TTFT and 2.8× the
P95 inter-token latency — and only at 1–2 concurrent streams, since MTP goes negative at 4+.
